# OpenPencil Capture 0.1.0

The first release of the extension.

- Pick any element on a page, or the whole page, and paste it into OpenPencil as editable layers: frames that keep the page's nesting, text in the fonts the browser drew with, SVG as vectors, gradients, images, shadows, borders, clip paths, masks and color filters.
- Captures open and closed shadow DOM, frames of the same site and of other sites, pseudo-elements and list markers, and `<use>` sprites from the page or from its own SVG files.
- The capture goes only to your clipboard, never to anyone else, and what the page hides or masks stays out. Images are downloaded again from the servers that host them, to embed them.

To install by hand, download **`openpencil-capture-0.1.0.zip`**, extract it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, and choose the extracted folder with **Load unpacked**. The same ZIP is the Chrome Web Store upload package.
